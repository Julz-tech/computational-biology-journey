## Terms
1. **Statistical learning**: Set of tools for understanding data and are classified into:
	1. **Supervised statistical learning**: building a statistical model for predicting an output based on one or more inputs. Includes tasks like:
		* **Classification**: Grouping data into distinct categories
		* **Regression**: Predicting a continuous numerical value
		**Example**
		* **Wage Data (regression)** 
			- Predicts wages (continuous/quantitative data) based on a variety of factors (age, year and education level)
		- **Stock Market Data**
			- Categorical/qualitative output
			- Predicts whether the index will **increase** or **decrease** on a given day, based on the past 5 day's percentage changes in the index
			- In other words, predicting whether whether a given day's stock market performance will fall into the up or down bucket (classification) 
	2. **Unsupervised statistical learning**: Learning structure from input data with no necessary output. Includes tasks like:
		* **Clustering**: Bundling data points based on shared similarities. E.g., _checking which types of customers are similar to each other by grouping individuals according to their observed characteristics_
		* **Association**: Finding the rules that link items together
		* **Dimensionality reduction**: Simplifying massive datasets while preserving crucial info
